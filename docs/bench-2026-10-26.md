# Bench, 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-26 — galvanostatic and impedance probes, on dummies

What the GITT (built 2026-10-29, `docs/data-format.md` §6) and the planned ER-EIS rest
on. Two probe scripts, run with dummy cells only:

- `examples/probe_gamry_gitt_eis.py`, on both Gamrys (Reference 600 and Interface 1010E)
  on 2026-10-23;
- `examples/probe_autolab_gitt_eis.py`, on the Autolab PGSTAT302N with its FRA32M:
  part 1 (read-only) on 2026-10-23, parts 2–4 on 2026-10-26.

The full reports are kept outside the repository.

**Short version:**
- **Galvanostatic control works on all three instruments**, and the Gamry can stop a
  pulse on the potential in the instrument itself. That is enough for the GITT.
- **Impedance works on both Gamrys.**
- **Impedance does NOT work on the Autolab, even from NOVA.** NOVA's own FRA32M module
  test fails, so it is a hardware or configuration fault, with Metrohm.

---

## 1. Galvanostatic pulses

### Gamry, both instruments (2026-10-23)

One array2 curve in `GSTATMODE`: 0, +I, 0, −I, 0 A, 3 s each, 0.1 s per point. The
Reference 600 was on the UDC4's Randles side (200 Ω + 3010 Ω ∥ 1 µF, 3210 Ω DC). The
1010E was on a 2 kΩ dummy.

| | I set | I measured | E measured | E / (I × R) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reference 600, +I | +31.15 µA | +31.10 µA | +0.10067 V | 1.007 |
| Reference 600, −I | −31.15 µA | −31.10 µA | −0.09886 V | 0.989 |
| Reference 600, 0 A | 0 | −0.9 to −1.1 nA | +0.87 mV | — |
| Interface 1010E, +I | +31.15 µA | +31.16 µA | +0.06237 V | 1.000 (on 2 kΩ) |
| Interface 1010E, 0 A | 0 | +0.33 to +0.35 nA | +0.04 mV | — |

- **`signal_array2_new(..., tkp.GSTATMODE)` takes AMPERES.** The current is applied
  within 0.2%.
- **The 0 A current is small on a Gamry:** about 1 nA, i.e. 1.7e-6 of the 600 µA range
  on the Reference 600 and 3.5e-7 of the 1 mA range on the 1010E. That is about 100×
  smaller, relative to full scale, than the Autolab's (below).
- **The Reference 600 reads +0.87 mV at 0 A into a resistor**; the 1010E +0.04 mV.
- **Range:** the probe asked for 3 × |I|, and the instrument took the rung at or above
  it (600 µA on the Reference 600, 1 mA on the 1010E). The GITT does the same.

**Can the instrument end a pulse on the potential?** A +I pulse with a stop at half of
I × R (+50 mV), stop delay 3 points:

| Curve | StopAt | Result, both instruments |
|---|---|---|
| `ChronoCurve` | `set_stop_x_max` | 30 of 30 points: **did NOT stop** (x is not the potential) |
| `CiivCurve` | `set_stop_v_max` + `set_stop_at_delay_v_max` | **4 of 30 points: STOPPED** |

So the GITT uses `CiivCurve`. `set_stop_v_min` was not tried. The test plan in TODO
includes it.

### Autolab PGSTAT302N (2026-10-26)

`Ei` in galvanostatic mode on a single 10 kΩ resistor, range `CR11_100uA`:

| | I set | Setpoint read back | I measured | E measured | E / (I × R) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +I | +10.000 µA | +9.9945 µA | +9.979 µA | +0.09976 V | 0.998 |
| −I | −10.000 µA | −9.9945 µA | −10.00 µA | −0.09988 V | 0.999 |
| 0 A | 0 | 0 | −15.6 to −16.2 nA | −0.06 to −0.20 mV | — |

- **In galvanostatic mode `Ei.Setpoint` is a current in amperes.** It reads back what
  the DAC took (9.9945 µA for 10), so a write is checked against a tolerance, never
  for equality.
- **The 0 A current is about −16 nA on `CR11_100uA`, i.e. 1.6e-4 of full scale.** That
  matches the +1.6 µA zero offset measured on `CR09_10mA` on 2026-09-11, also 1.6e-4
  of full scale. Over a long relaxation it moves a real fraction of a small pulse's
  charge, so the GITT's Start warns when it matters.
- **There is no instrument-side potential stop in this route.** The GITT checks the
  window in Python on every sample.

## 2. Impedance

### Gamry, both instruments (2026-10-23) — works

`tkp.ReadZ(pstat)` with `readz.measure(f, vac, vdc)`, one call per frequency, and
`ZCurve` (`FraCurve` is not used). The 1010E needs no licence for it.

| f (Hz) | Reference 600 on the UDC4 Randles: \|Z\| error | phase error | s per point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 000 | +0.5% | 0.0° | 0.31 |
| 1 000 | +0.2% | +0.1° | 0.31 |
| 100 | −0.7% | 0.0° | 0.32 |
| 10 | −0.1% | 0.0° | 0.46 |
| 1 | 0.0% | +0.1° | 3.09 |

- **The 1010E read a flat 1998–2000 Ω** from 10 kHz to 1 Hz. It was on the 2 kΩ dummy,
  not the Randles, so that is right (1 Hz took 4.2 s).
- **Upper frequency limit:** 1 MHz on the Reference 600, 2 MHz on the 1010E.
- **Filters:** the PITT's 1 kHz channel filters did not change the 10 kHz point.

**The trap for ER-EIS: ReadZ leaves the current range where it autoranged.** It was 8 →
6 on the Reference 600 and 9 → 7 on the 1010E, and it also changes the I-channel
filter. A DC hold after the sweep then clips at about 109% of that range's full scale,
every point identical, with no error:

| Hold at +0.1 V | before the sweep | after | after restoring the range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reference 600 | 32.04 µA | 6.554 µA (all 20 points) | 31.50 µA |
| Interface 1010E | 50.20 µA | 10.94 µA (all 20 points) | 49.82 µA |

The I-channel offset was never on. **An ER-EIS runner must restore `ie_range` and the
filters after every sweep.** The ZCurve fields include `vmod` and `imod`, which could
settle whether the amplitude is rms or peak. That has not been checked.

### Autolab PGSTAT302N with FRA32M (2026-10-23 and 2026-10-26) — does NOT work

**What the SDK has (part 1, read-only):**
- `GetFraModule()` returns the FRA32M, connected and initialized.
- **`Instrument.Fra`** (`FraManualControlCommand`) does one frequency at a time:
  - settings: `Frequency`, `Amplitude` (default 0.1), `WaveType`,
    `MinimumIntegrationTime` 1 s;
  - `Start()`, which blocks for 2.5–7.4 s per point;
  - result arrays `Real` / `Imaginary` / `Modulus` / `Phase` / `Frequecies` (the
    vendor's spelling).
- **The SDK's one standard impedance procedure** is `FRA impedance potentiostatic.nox`:
  - 50 frequencies, 100 kHz to 0.1 Hz, 251 s;
  - its `FIAMeasurement` parameters: [0] frequency, [1] amplitude 0.01, [3] "V";
  - its results (`H_Real`, `H_Imaginary`, ...) fill in DURING the sweep.

**On the 10 kΩ resistor every route gave noise:** the manual `Instrument.Fra`, the
`.nox` run from Python, and NOVA itself.

**NOVA's own module test (TestFRA) FAILED** on its required dummy setup, DummyCell 2
circuit (c), 100 Ω + (1 kΩ ∥ 1 µF):
- At 10 mV that circuit should carry about 9–100 µA of AC current. TestFRA saw about
  1 µA, and about 50 µV of AC potential.
- Both channels are near zero, so the AC signal most likely never reaches the cell.
- NOVA's "FRA32M input" switch (Advanced) was found off. Turning it on did not help,
  and it was switched back off.
- **The front-panel X, Y and V BNCs are not the cause.** Per the FRA32M manual they are
  for external transfer functions only. Do not wire them to the monitor BNCs.

The failed TestFRA data is saved for Metrohm. **Autolab ER-EIS is blocked until it
passes.**

After that, rerun on the PGSTAT302N:
- NOVA on 10 kΩ;
- the probe's `--fra`, `--fra --fra-vdc 0.1` and `--fra-manual`;
- circuit (c) (`--rs 100 --rp 1000 --c 1e-6`), to settle the sign convention.

DummyCell 2, for reference:
- (a) 100 Ω + (1 MΩ ∥ 1 µF), via CE;
- (b) 100 Ω + (1 MΩ ∥ 1 µF);
- (c) 100 Ω + (1 kΩ ∥ 1 µF);
- (d) 100 Ω + 1 µF + 5 kΩ in series;
- (e) 10 kΩ.

## 3. What this decided

- **GITT on the Gamry:** one `CiivCurve` per step (`[I] × n` then `[0 A]`), so the
  instrument times the pulse and stops it on the potential. The DAC is zeroed before
  cell-on, and the range is 3 × |I|.
- **GITT on the Autolab:** `Ei` galvanostatic, with Python ending the pulse and
  checking the window. Start warns about the 0 A offset when it is large next to the
  pulse.
- **ER-EIS** stays a plan. It is ready on the Gamry, provided the range and filters are
  restored after each sweep, and blocked on the Autolab.
- **Not yet done:**
  - a GITT on a slow capacitor dummy (the UDC4's 1 µF settles in 3 ms, too fast to
    see at 0.1 s; the plan in TODO uses about 1000 µF);
  - `set_stop_v_min`;
  - the amplitude units.
