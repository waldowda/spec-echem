  The driver's autolab_dio_port indexes this list.

  MEASURED 2026-09-09: five ports per connector — Port_A, Port_B, Port_C,
  Port_C_Upper, Port_C_Lower — on each of P1 and P2. A different INDEX is
  therefore NOT automatically an independent line: C_Upper and C_Lower are
  the two nibbles OF Port_C, so driving C also drives both, and driving
  either moves half of C. Independent pairs are A/B/C within a connector,
  and anything on P2 versus anything on P1.
