A — what one data point costs (no cell)
========================================================================
  500 trials. pump() does Sample() + two latch reads per spectrum,
  and acquisition budgets SPECTRUM_OVERHEAD_S = 30 ms for everything that is
  not exposure. If a point costs more than that, pump() is now the overhead.

    Sampler.Sample() alone median  24.983 ms   min 23.895   p95  25.604   max  29.719
    Ei.Potential (latch)   median   4.997 ms   min  4.322   p95   5.271   max   5.982
    Ei.Current (latch)     median   5.000 ms   min  4.366   p95   5.267   max   5.692
    FULL POINT (what pump does) median  34.991 ms   min 34.132   p95  35.385   max  35.720

  One point = 34.99 ms median.
  Against the 30 ms overhead budget: 117% of it.
  Against a 100 ms grid: 35.0% of the slot.

  -> pump() is now a significant cost and SPECTRUM_OVERHEAD_S understates
     the per-spectrum overhead. THIS is the cadence-outlier suspect.
